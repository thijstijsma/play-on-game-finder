PLAY ON · 200

Every game on the HEMA retro handheld (art. 39640203). Find the picture, read out the number, scroll to it in the menu.

The menu order is the same on every HEMA 200-in-1. Check that game 1 is F-22 and game 23 is Vigilant; if so, this list matches your device.

Pictures and game info come from the BootlegGames Wiki and the libretro thumbnail library; for games found nowhere else, a frame from a gameplay video. Some pictures show a game's title screen instead of the game itself. Many games on this device are copies of older games with a new name; for some of those the picture comes from the original game, so colours or characters can differ slightly. Games marked "best guess" have no documentation, so their description comes from the name only.
